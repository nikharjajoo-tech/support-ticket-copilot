# KB01 · Accounts, plans and sign-up

**Plans:** Nimbus CRM has three plans: **Free**, **Gold** ($25/user/month) and **Platinum** ($60/user/month). Plans are billed monthly or annually (annual = 2 months free).

**Create an account / add a user:** Account admins add users in **Settings → Team → Invite user**. Each user needs their own email address. A new user can be added to any paid plan. For a different company, create a separate workspace at nimbuscrm.example/signup.

**Change plan (upgrade or downgrade):** Admins change plan in **Settings → Billing → Change plan**. Upgrades take effect immediately (prorated). Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period. Downgrading to Free removes paid features and keeps data read-only for 90 days.

**Update account details:** Name, email and company details are edited in **Settings → Profile**. Email changes need confirmation from both the old and new address.

**Sign-up problems:** Most sign-up errors are caused by (1) an email already registered, so use **Forgot password**, or (2) the verification email going to spam. Verification links expire after 24 hours. If neither fixes it, contact support with a screenshot of the error.

**Delete an account:** Only the workspace admin can delete it, in **Settings → Workspace → Delete workspace**. Data is kept for a 30-day grace period and can be restored by contacting support; after 30 days it is permanently deleted. Deletion is blocked while an annual contract is active (see KB06).
