# KB02 · Invoices

**Find and download invoices:** Admins and billing contacts can view and download every invoice as a PDF in **Settings → Billing → Invoices**. Search by invoice number (e.g. #12588) or date.

**Invoice timing:** Invoices are issued on the first day of each billing period and emailed to the billing contact.

**Change invoice details** (company name, address, tax/VAT ID): edit in **Settings → Billing → Billing details**. Changes apply to future invoices. To reissue a past invoice with corrected details, contact support with the invoice number; reissues take up to 3 business days.

**Can't see an invoice:** Only admins and billing contacts can see invoices. Ask your workspace admin to add you as a billing contact.
