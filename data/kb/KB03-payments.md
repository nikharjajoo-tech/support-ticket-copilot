# KB03 · Payment methods and payment problems

**Accepted payment methods:** Visa, Mastercard, American Express and PayPal for all plans. **Bank transfer (wire/ACH)** is available only on annual plans of $5,000 or more. We do not accept cheques or cryptocurrency.

**Update payment method:** **Settings → Billing → Payment method**.

**Payment failed:** Common causes are an expired card, insufficient funds, or the bank blocking an international charge. Update the card or contact your bank, then click **Retry payment** in Settings → Billing. After a failed payment, the account stays fully active for a **7-day grace period** before it is limited to read-only.

**Charged twice or wrong amount:** Contact support with the invoice number. Billing errors are investigated within 2 business days, and any overcharge is refunded to the original payment method.
