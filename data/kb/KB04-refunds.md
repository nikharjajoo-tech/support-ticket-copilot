# KB04 · Refunds

**Refund policy:** Monthly plans can be refunded in full within **14 days** of the charge. Annual plans can be refunded in full within **30 days** of purchase; after 30 days, annual plans are not refundable. Charges from billing errors are always refunded (see KB03).

**How to request a refund:** Admins request a refund in **Settings → Billing → Invoices → Request refund** next to the relevant invoice, or by contacting support with the invoice number.

**Refund status and timing:** Approved refunds are processed within **5 business days** and then take **5–10 business days** to appear, depending on your bank. Status is shown next to the invoice in Settings → Billing → Invoices. If a refund has not arrived 15 business days after approval, contact support with the invoice number.
