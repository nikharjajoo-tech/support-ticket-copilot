# KB05 · Newsletter and product emails

**Subscribe:** Sign up for the monthly Nimbus newsletter at nimbuscrm.example/newsletter or in **Settings → Notifications → Newsletter**.

**Unsubscribe:** Click **Unsubscribe** at the bottom of any newsletter email, or turn it off in **Settings → Notifications → Newsletter**. Changes take effect within 48 hours. Unsubscribing does not affect account or billing emails, which cannot be turned off.
