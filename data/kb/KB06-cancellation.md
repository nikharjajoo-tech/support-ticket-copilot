# KB06 · Cancellation and early exit fees

**Monthly plans:** Cancel anytime in **Settings → Billing → Cancel subscription**. There is **no cancellation fee**. Access continues until the end of the paid month.

**Annual plans:** Can be cancelled at any time, but cancelling before the end of the 12-month term incurs an **early exit fee equal to 2 months of your plan's price** (for all users on the plan). Cancelling within the first 30 days is fee-free and fully refunded (see KB04).

**Where to see your fee:** The exact early exit fee is shown in **Settings → Billing → Cancel subscription** before you confirm. Nothing is charged until you confirm.

**Before you cancel:** You can downgrade instead (see KB01) or talk to your account manager about options.
