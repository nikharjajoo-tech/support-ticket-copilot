# KB07 · Contacting support

**Hours:** Live chat and phone support are available **Monday–Friday, 8:00–20:00 (GMT)**. Email support (support@nimbuscrm.example) is answered 24/7, with a first reply within **4 business hours** for paid plans and **2 business days** for the Free plan.

**Talk to a person:** Open the chat widget (bottom-right in the app) and type "agent" to reach a human during live hours. Platinum customers can also call their dedicated account manager.

**Outside hours:** Chat messages sent outside live hours are converted to email tickets and answered next business day.
