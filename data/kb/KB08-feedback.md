# KB08 · Reviews, feedback and complaints

**Leave a review or product feedback:** Use **Help → Give feedback** in the app, or the feedback form at nimbuscrm.example/feedback. Feedback goes directly to the product team.

**Make a formal complaint:** Submit a complaint at nimbuscrm.example/complaints or email complaints@nimbuscrm.example. Every complaint gets a reference number, an acknowledgement within **2 business days**, and a written response within **10 business days**. If you're not satisfied with the response, you can ask for it to be escalated to the Head of Customer Experience.
